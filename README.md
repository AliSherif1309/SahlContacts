## ✨ Highlights

| Feature | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| 🔍 **Arabic-Smart Search** | Matches أ/ا/إ, ة/ه, and ى/ي automatically and supports both Arabic and English digits. |
| 🔒 **Biometric Hidden Vault** | Keep private contacts protected behind your fingerprint or screen lock. |
